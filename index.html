Overview

RoboGrip is a manually controlled, high-mobility robot engineered for precise object handling, obstacle navigation, and ramp climbing. It earned second position at IIT Bombay Techfest 2025.

Key Features

  • 4WD mobility: Brushed motors provide strong traction and stability on challenging terrain, ramps, and inclined surfaces.
  • Precision pick and place: A waterproof, high-torque servo drives a mechanical claw that grips, lifts, and places objects accurately.
  • Wireless real-time control: A wireless transmitter and receiver give the operator responsive manual control.
  • ESC motor control: Electronic Speed Controllers deliver smooth, proportional acceleration and direction changes.
  • Competition-ready design: A compact build balances maneuverability, stability, and payload handling.
  • High-power battery system: A LiPo/Li-ion battery system powers the motors, with regulated power for the control electronics.
  • Arduino-based control: An Arduino coordinates the ESCs and servo, turning wireless commands into movement and gripping.
