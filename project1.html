Overview

RoboGrip is a manually controlled robot built for precise object handling, obstacle navigation, and ramp climbing. Its strong drive system, accurate gripper, and responsive wireless control earned it second position at IIT Bombay Techfest 2025.

Key Features

4WD Mobility

Strong traction and stability on ramps and rough arena terrain.

Precision Grip

A high-torque, waterproof servo claw that lifts and places objects accurately.

Wireless Control

Responsive manual control through a wireless transmitter and receiver.

Smooth Drive

Electronic speed controllers give proportional acceleration and clean direction changes.

Reliable Power

A LiPo/Li-ion battery system with regulated power for the electronics.

Arduino Brain

Turns every wireless command into precise movement and gripping.

Built For

Pick & Place CompetitionsRamp ClimbingPrecision ManeuveringComplex Arena Navigation

Engineering Focus

Embedded systems, wireless communication, motor control, and mechanical design come together in one competition-ready platform, balancing mobility, grip precision, stability, and operator control.
